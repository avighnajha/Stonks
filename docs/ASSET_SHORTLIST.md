## How this connects to experiments

The catalogue can contain all the names we like; each research run selects its own subset, currently up to 10 assets. A larger catalogue does not require trading all assets in every experiment.

Sector/subsector labels help organise assets and configure shared simulated factors. They do not establish empirical real-world correlations. For example, two football assets can share a simulated football factor, while an asset-specific event affects only one. The chosen factor weights, information and trader behaviour determine the experiment.

Potential comparisons include Irony versus Sincerity, Hustle culture versus Work-life balance, and AI agents versus Human-made. These suggest questions for scenarios; opposite names do not create automatic negative correlations.

For initial strategy development, start with one asset, then a related pair and an unrelated pair. The larger catalogue supplies recognisable choices once that baseline works.
